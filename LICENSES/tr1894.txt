Historical Note:
F. H. A. Scrivener (1813-1891) produced this edition to reproduce
the Greek text underlying the 1611 King James Version. He used the
Beza 1598 edition as his starting point, examining eighteen editions
of the Textus Receptus to determine the correct Greek rendering.
